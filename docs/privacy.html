Tickerline — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Tickerline is a Chrome extension that draws a text and logo strip over your own camera video in Google Meet.

The short version

Tickerline has no server. It makes no network requests. Nothing you type into it leaves your computer, at all.

What is stored, and where

Tickerline stores the settings you configure: the text on each bar, colours, position and size, the chosen template, and the logo image you choose.

These are written to Chrome's extension storage on your own computer (chrome.storage.local). They stay there until you change them or remove the extension.

What is not collected

  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or usage statistics.
  • No account, no sign-in, no identifiers.
  • No video or audio is recorded, stored, or transmitted. The extension draws onto
  • No browsing history or data from any other site. The extension runs only on
  • Nothing is sold or shared with third parties, because nothing is collected.

Camera video

Tickerline modifies your outgoing camera frames inside your own browser so the strip appears on them. Those frames go to Google Meet exactly as they otherwise would. The extension never sends video anywhere itself and never retains frames.

Permissions, and why

  • storage — to keep your settings between calls.
  • **https://meet.google.com/*** — to run on Google Meet and draw the strip.

Removing your data

Removing the extension from chrome://extensions deletes its settings. There is nothing stored anywhere else to remove.
